# 🧪 Experiments

These are fun little test scripts and exploratory tools. They might not fit neatly into a category, but they’re still useful or interesting in their own right.

### 📝 Scripts

- **`get_screen.py`**  
  Lets you take a screenshot of a defined area on your screen — handy for quick visual captures.

- **`get_pointer_coordinates.py`**  
  Shows your mouse pointer's position in real-time. Useful when you’re trying to automate clicks or track movement.

---
These scripts are more experimental in nature — try them out, break them, improve them!
